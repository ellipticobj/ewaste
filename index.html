Environmental & Human Impact

Toxic Soil & Water

Lead, mercury, and cadmium from discarded electronics leach into groundwater and soil, poisoning ecosystems for generations.

Air Pollution

Burning e-waste releases toxic fumes including dioxins and furans, causing respiratory diseases in nearby communities.

Child Labor

Millions of children in developing countries work in informal e-waste recycling, exposed to hazardous materials daily.

Lost Resources

Less than 20% of global e-waste is recycled, squandering precious metals like gold, silver, and rare earth elements.
